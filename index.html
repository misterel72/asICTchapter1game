Your field guide

Use Tab to move, Enter or Space to activate. Inspect evidence, build an answer and test it. There is no countdown. Hints and retries are always available.

Each solved puzzle earns 100 XP, minus 10 per earlier unsuccessful test and 10 for a hint, with a minimum of 50 XP. XP is a game reward, not a mark or grade.

Exam command words

These are revision guides. Follow the wording, context and mark allocation of the actual exam. The game practises the thinking behind these words, not full written exam responses.

Progress stays in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes it. Nothing is sent to a teacher or Google Sheet. On a shared computer, use “Clear my progress” when finished.
